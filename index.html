Camping Adventures

Our troop goes on exciting camping trips throughout the year. Below are two of our regular camp destinations with addresses and packing guidance.

🏕️ Camp Shands

Next Trip: TBD

Location: Camp Shands, Melrose, FL

Address: 1453 Baden Powell Rd, Hawthorne, FL 32640

Overview: Camp Shands is known for its lake aquatics — canoeing and swimming — plus merit-badge sessions in a wooded, spring-fed-lake environment.

Suggested Gear

  • Sleeping bag & sleeping pad
  • Tent (if not provided) and footprint
  • Weather appropriate clothing + rain jacket
  • Swimwear & water shoes (for lake activities)
  • Personal hygiene kit, towel, sunscreen, insect repellent
  • Water bottle (refillable), headlamp, pocketknife (if allowed)

🌲 Camp Echockotee

Next Trip: TBD

Location: Camp Echockotee, Orange Park, FL

Address: 2513 Doctors Lake Dr, Orange Park, FL 32073

Overview: Camp Echockotee features high-adventure programming, a climbing tower, zip line, and wilderness skills courses for older scouts.

Suggested Gear

  • Sleeping bag & pad, sturdy hiking boots
  • Helmet & harness (if provided by camp for climbing — verify before packing)
  • Clothing layers for changeable weather and overnight temps
  • Gloves for rappelling/ropes work, long pants for tower activities
  • Flashlight/headlamp, first-aid kit, personal medications
  • Water bottle, snacks, pocketknife (if permitted), sunscreen, insect repellent
